Critters: big crit popups, a Crit Streak and a permanent best-crit tracker for WotLK 3.3.5a



Critters turns every critical hit into a moment. Your normal crits pop up as animated numbers (pop, drop, slide or float), and the best crit of each fight slides up out of a Crit Streak counter with its own effect (smash, drop-in, slide-in, bouncy, cascade or heartbeat). All numbers are built from separate characters and tilted, since 3.3.5 can't rotate text.



What it does in game



Crit Streak. Crits of 30% or more of your all-time record start a streak. The big number is all the damage and healing you do while it runs. Each further big crit pops it and raises a counter that turns from white at x1 to red at x20. It slides away after 5 seconds without a big crit.



Record crit. Your best crit of the current fight appears above the streak. It can then fly to a permanent spot on screen, bonking the old number away.



Highest Crits. A permanent best-crit number for damage and one for healing. It can show your best crit since you joined the current group or raid ("In Group"), your all-time best ("Always"), or nothing ("Never"). Hover it for the spell, target and target health %.



All-time list. An account-wide table of every character's best damage and heal crit. Shift+left-click a row to share it to Say, Yell, Party, Raid or a whisper, with a clickable spell link.



Fair records. Crits made under the Blood Queen Lana'thel buff and Valithria's healing buffs can be left out of your all-time records. You can turn this off.



Customisation. Separate fonts, sizes, digit spacing and colours for each element, a custom font for the game's own combat text (needs a relog), movable elements with a one-click reset to screen centre, a resizable options window, and a minimap button.



Slash commands: /critters or /crit.
